About

Hi, I'm Chana!

Founder of Mamanew · ICEA Certified Childbirth Educator

I'm a mom, a certified childbirth educator, and someone who deeply believes that the way a woman experiences birth matters just as much as the birth itself.

My passion for childbirth education began with my own journey into motherhood. Through my personal birth experience, I discovered just how powerful the right support can be. Having someone who made me feel safe, heard, informed, and supported didn't just change my experience in the moment — it changed the way that birth lives in my memory, my mind, and my body.

That realization stayed with me.

I began to understand that birth isn't simply a medical event. It's a deeply personal, physical, and emotional experience that can shape the way a woman sees herself, her body, and her transition into motherhood.

And I knew I wanted to be part of making that experience a positive one for other women.

That's why I created Mamanew.

As an ICEA Certified Childbirth Educator, my goal is to bridge the gap between knowledge and confidence. I believe that when women understand their bodies, know their options, and feel empowered to make informed decisions, they can approach birth with greater confidence and less fear.

My classes go beyond simply explaining the stages of labor. They're about helping you understand what's happening, preparing you for the unexpected, giving you practical tools, and helping you and your support person feel ready to navigate birth together.

I don't believe there's one perfect way to give birth. I believe in informed choices, compassionate support, and making sure every woman feels like an active participant in her own birth experience.

Because while we can't always control how birth unfolds, we can change how prepared, supported, and empowered a woman feels throughout it.

And that's what Mamanew is all about.

Helping you enter birth informed, supported, and confident — and motherhood feeling empowered.

The course

What you'll walk away with

Everything you need to know for labor, birth, and the days after, taught in a way that feels calm, clear, and never clinical.

Birth Knowledge

The stages of labor, what's happening in your body, common procedures, and your options.

Pain Management

An honest look at every option, from natural approaches to medical pain relief.

Comfort Techniques

Breathing, movement, positioning, massage, counterpressure, and other hands-on tools.

Medical Interventions

What interventions are, why they're offered, and the questions to ask.

Emotional Coping

Techniques to navigate stress, uncertainty, intensity, and the demands of labor.

Communication & Advocacy

Ask informed questions, share your preferences, and collaborate with your care team.

Partner Preparation

Practical guidance so your partner can recognize your needs and truly support you.

Postpartum

Recovery, the early days with your newborn, and setting up the support you'll need.
