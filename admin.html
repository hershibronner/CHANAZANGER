Payments & prices

Where the "Complete payment" buttons on your site send people.

How to get a payment link (Stripe, Square or PayPal all work):
  1. In Stripe, go to Payment Links → New.
  2. Add a product, e.g. "Group Birth Class", with the price.
  3. Under After payment, choose to show a confirmation page.
  4. Copy the link and paste it above.
If a link is left empty, moms still reserve their spot, and you'll see them as unpaid here so you can follow up.
